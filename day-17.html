Session 1 — Map the appointment lifecycle

≈ 45 minutes

For a clinic, no-shows are pure lost revenue — the chair sits empty either way. The fix is a message system anchored to the appointment's status and start time, not to when the contact enrolled. GHL models this with the Appointment Status trigger and the special Wait → until an event/appointment time option.

Session 2 — Workflow A: "Appointment Confirmations & Reminders"

≈ 2.5 hours

The heart of today. Key new skill: the Wait action's "until appointment time" mode, which parks the contact until a moment relative to their booking — so reminders fire at exactly 24 h and 1 h before, no matter when they booked.

Session 3 — Workflow B: "No-Show Recovery" + Workflow C: "Post-Visit"

≈ 2 hours

Two shorter builds. B chases the money the clinic almost lost; C banks the goodwill (and sets up Day 19's review machine).

Session 4 — Drive the lifecycle end to end + package Portfolio #6

≈ 1.5 hours

You'll test by doing what a front-desk person does all day: booking an appointment and manually changing its status. Manual status changes fire the same triggers as real life — this is the standard way pros test appointment automations.
