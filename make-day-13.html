≈ 2 hours

Certification won't get you hired alone, but it gets you past filters, and recruiters searching for Make talent do look for it. After twelve days of building, the Foundation material is mostly review — which is exactly why today is the day to bank it.

≈ 1 hour

📦 Deliverable of the day

Three timed-drill blueprints with recorded times and retros (all three passing, even if on attempt two), the Make Foundation certification earned and screenshotted, an Intermediate exam date in the calendar, a clean consistently-named blueprint library verified by re-import, and the Day 1 vs Day 13 operations comparison written up in the journal.

💡 The 30-minute claim, kept honest

Only claim the speed you clocked. If Drill 3 took you 40 minutes, say "under 45" — then drill it weekly until it's 25. Interviewers occasionally test these claims live; the candidates who survive are the ones whose numbers came from a real timer, not vibes.
