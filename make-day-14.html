Session 1 — A Loom for every build

≈ 2.5 hours

One script, seven recordings. The script: (1) the problem in business terms, 20 seconds — (2) live demo, 60–90 seconds, the thing actually running — (3) design choices, 30 seconds, ALWAYS including operations cost and error handling — (4) what a client gets, 15 seconds: blueprint, documentation, handover. Two to three minutes, no more.

Session 2 — Case studies for M3, M4, M7

≈ 1.5 hours

A case study is one page, and it is honest. Template — five headings: The problem (2–3 sentences, generic business framing) · The build (module chain + the pattern it demonstrates) · Design choices (why this trigger, operations cost math, error handling) · What this demonstrates (the skills, framed as a demonstration build) · Assets (blueprint file, screenshots, Loom link).
