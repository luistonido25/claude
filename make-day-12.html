Day 12Portfolio M7: Content Pipeline with Approval Step

Your final Phase 2 build is the one clients ask about most in 2026: AI drafts content, but a HUMAN approves it before anything ships. You'll generate post drafts with OpenAI from an ideas sheet (or an RSS feed), park each draft in a Data store, send it to Telegram with tappable Approve/Reject links, and catch the click with a webhook that routes the decision. Human-in-the-loop is the pattern — and yours will be built the portable way.

0% of today's tasks complete

Session 1 — Source + AI draft generator

≈ 2 hours

Primary source is an Ideas sheet — deterministic and cheap to test. You'll swap in the RSS module as a variant at the end. The AI's job is narrow: rewrite an idea into a post draft under strict brand-voice rules. Narrow prompts are testable prompts.

Session 2 — The approval message: Telegram with two live links

≈ 1.5 hours

The approval "buttons" are just links to a webhook, with the decision and the draft ID in the query string. You need the webhook URL before you can compose the message — so you'll create the second scenario's trigger first, then come back.

Session 3 — The approval handler: webhook → data store → router → response

≈ 2 hours

One scenario handles both outcomes. The webhook receives a GET when a link is tapped; query-string parameters arrive as mappable fields. Every route ends with a Webhook response so the person clicking sees confirmation in their browser — small touch, very client-impressive.

Session 4 — Full test, RSS variant, packaging

≈ 1.5 hours
