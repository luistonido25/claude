Session 1 — Why speed-to-lead is the money automation

≈ 1 hour

Local businesses lose leads for one dominant reason: they respond too slowly. Studies show that responding within about 5 minutes massively lifts your odds of actually reaching and qualifying a lead compared to waiting even 30 minutes — and most businesses take hours. Automating the first touch is therefore the easiest ROI story an agency can sell. You're building that story today.

Session 2 — Build the "Speed to Lead" workflow

≈ 3 hours

Build slowly and deliberately. Every action gets a clear name, every message uses merge fields, and you save after every step. Work inside the Summit Dental sub-account.

Session 3 — Missed-call text-back

≈ 1 hour

The second half of every speed-to-lead pitch: when the clinic misses a phone call, the caller instantly gets a text. GHL ships this as a dedicated feature — no workflow needed.

Session 4 — End-to-end test, evidence & portfolio packaging

≈ 1.5–2 hours

Now prove the whole machine works the way a REAL lead would experience it: through the public form, not by clicking Test.

⚠️ Watch out

If Sally never enrolled: 90% of the time it's one of these — the workflow was left in Draft, the trigger filter points at a different form than the one you submitted, or you edited the form and submitted an old cached version. Check those three before anything else. And never "test" by re-submitting with the same email while Re-Entry is off mid-debug — you'll think it's broken when it's just refusing re-entry.
