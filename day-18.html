Session 1 — If/Else mastery: branches & condition types

≈ 1.5 hours

You've used If/Else with one condition. Real builds branch three, four, five ways on combinations of fields, tags, and activity. Work in a fresh practice workflow in the Training folder — today is a lab day; only the final drip is portfolio-adjacent.

Session 2 — Waits, Goals, Go To, Split & AI actions

≈ 2.5 hours

Five power tools, one session. The star is the Goal event — the mechanic behind every "stop messaging them once they book" system, and the single most impressive thing you can name-drop in a GHL interview.

Session 3 — Sending discipline & the failure catalog

≈ 1.5 hours

Advanced builders are defined less by what they can build than by what they can DEBUG. This session builds your failure catalog — the checklist you'll run every time someone says "the automation isn't working."

Session 4 — Lab: "Lead Nurture 7-Day" drip with a conversion goal

≈ 2 hours

The graduation exercise: a 7-day nurture drip where booking a consult is a Goal that instantly pulls converts out of the message queue. You'll run two contacts through it — one converts, one doesn't — and compare their logs. (For testing, we compress "days" into minutes; the structure is identical.)

📦 Deliverable of the day

A published "Lead Nurture 7-Day" drip in Client Systems with a working conversion Goal — proven by two contrasting Execution Log runs (Connie converts and skips ahead; Nolan rides the full drip) — three screenshots (day18-*.png), a booking_link custom value wired into your emails, and a journal failure-catalog you'll use for the rest of your career.

💡 Pro tip

The compressed-test pattern you used today — build with 2-minute waits, verify in logs, then switch to production durations — is how professionals test ANY long-running automation. Never ship a 7-day sequence you've only watched for 7 seconds, and never wait 7 days to find a typo in Email 3.
