Session 1 — The contact record, inside and out

≈ 1.5 hours

Everything in GHL hangs off the contact record: every email, SMS, appointment, opportunity, and automation is attached to a contact. Enter Summit Dental via the account switcher — today never leaves this sub-account.

Session 2 — Custom fields & tags: design your data model

≈ 2 hours

Before importing 20 patients, build the structure they'll land in. Rule of thumb you should memorize: a custom field stores an attribute with one current value per contact ("Insurance Provider: Delta Dental"); a tag is a stackable yes/no label used for segmenting and triggering automations ("vip", "new-lead"). Fields answer "what is true about this person?", tags answer "which buckets is this person in?".

Session 3 — Build a practice CSV and import it

≈ 2 hours

Importing lists is a bread-and-butter task for GHL specialists — clients constantly hand over spreadsheets from old systems. You'll build a 20-row patient CSV, then import it with proper field mapping.

Session 4 — Smart Lists, bulk actions, DND & duplicates

≈ 1.5 hours

A Smart List is a saved, live-updating filter — the backbone of segmentation. Build the three views a dental office would actually use.

📦 Deliverable of the day

Summit Dental now holds 25 clean contacts imported with correct field mapping, three custom fields in a Patient Info folder, four standard tags, three saved Smart Lists (Lapsed Patients, New Leads This Month, VIP Patients), plus two screenshots in your portfolio folder and a Day 2 journal entry with the tag-vs-field rule in your own words.

💡 Pro tip

Keep summit-dental-patients.csv forever. On Day 7 you'll re-import it into a test account under time pressure, and in job interviews "I can build and import a mapped CSV in five minutes" is a concrete, checkable claim — most applicants can't.

⚠️ Watch out

Never put real people's emails or phone numbers in practice data. Stick to @example.com addresses and +1 555 01XX XXXX numbers. On Day 4 you'll connect real sending channels, and stray real addresses in a test account can lead to accidental sends — a fireable offense in a real job.
