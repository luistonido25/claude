Session 1 — Account, UI tour, and the Make mental model

≈ 1.5 hours

Make (formerly Integromat) is a visual automation platform. Its unit of work is the scenario — a chain of modules that runs on a schedule or instantly via webhooks. Unlike n8n, you pay per operation: every time any module executes once, that's one operation off your quota.

Session 2 — Build your first scenario, click by click

≈ 2 hours

The classic starter: a scheduled scenario that reads a Google Sheet and emails you a summary. Small, but it exercises connections, mapping, and scheduling.

Session 3 — Blueprints, templates, and lock-in drills

≈ 2 hours

📦 Deliverable of the day

A live, scheduled scenario ([TRAIN] Daily sheet digest v1) with a filter, its exported blueprint in your portfolio folder, two screenshots, a written operations-cost explanation, and (for n8n users) a completed translation table.

💡 Operations discipline

Turn the scenario switch OFF when you finish each day's work. A forgotten every-15-minutes schedule can eat a free plan's 1,000 operations in a weekend — and telling a client "I burned your quota testing" is not a portfolio story.
