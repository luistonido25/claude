Session 1 — Pipeline-driven automation (both directions)

≈ 2 hours

Pipelines and workflows talk to each other in BOTH directions: a stage change can trigger a workflow, and a workflow action can move an opportunity to a new stage. Master both and you can automate an entire sales process. Today's scenario: when a Summit Dental consult ends in an accepted treatment plan, the machine kicks off the paperwork.

Session 2 — Internal ops: notifications 3 ways, tasks & round-robin

≈ 1.5 hours

Client-facing messages win the contract; INTERNAL automation keeps it. This session is about the staff side: every alert channel, and automatic lead distribution across a team.

Session 3 — Webhooks: your bridge to Week 4

≈ 2.5 hours

Read this paragraph twice — it's the doorway to your Week 4 superpowers. A webhook is one system sending an automatic HTTP message to another system's URL the moment something happens — like a phone call between apps, where the "conversation" is a chunk of structured data called JSON. GHL can both send webhooks (the Webhook ACTION — data goes OUT to any URL when a workflow step runs) and receive them (the Inbound Webhook TRIGGER — an outside system starts a GHL workflow). Every GHL↔n8n integration you build next week is just these two doors used well.
