📦 Deliverable of the day

Both directions of the sync live and verified against the three-case test matrix (new row → contact; edited contact → row; guarded row-edit push), a tested error-alert workflow, three workflow JSONs in GHL Portfolio/n8n-exports, four screenshots, the Loom, and PASS/FAIL matrix results in your journal.

⚠️ Watch out

Never match rows by email in a long-lived sync — emails change and duplicates creep in; the Contact ID is the only stable key. And if you ever see executions firing every few seconds, deactivate both workflows first, then investigate — that's the loop signature, and it can burn through API rate limits and n8n execution quotas fast.
