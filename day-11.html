Session 4 — Domains explained + SEO pass

≈ 1.5 hours

You will NOT buy a domain today — the free preview URL is fine for the whole program. But you must be able to explain domain connection fluently, because it's a day-one task in almost every GHL job.

📦 Deliverable of the day

A 4-page Ironworks Fitness website with working navigation and a global header/footer, a published blog post with full SEO settings, an SEO title/description on every page, a rehearsed 60-second domain-connection explanation in your journal, and 3 screenshots in your portfolio folder.

⚠️ Watch out

Do not point any real domain's DNS at GHL "just to try it" if that domain currently runs a live website or email — a wrong A record can take a site down. This is exactly why agencies use a fresh subdomain for GHL. In this program, the preview URL is all you need.
