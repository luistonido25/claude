Session 1 — Plan the offer & make the lead magnet

≈ 1.5 hours

Rookie mistake: opening the page editor with no offer. Pros plan the exchange first — the visitor gives their contact info, and gets something genuinely useful back. Ironworks' lead magnet: a free "Gym Starter Guide" PDF.

Session 2 — Build the form + Step 1: the opt-in page

≈ 2.5 hours

Build the form first, then the page that hosts it. An opt-in page has one job and therefore one possible action — no nav bar, no footer links, nothing to click except the form.

Session 3 — Step 2: thank-you page, wiring & tagging

≈ 2 hours

The thank-you page is where amateurs stop and pros start: it confirms the win, delivers the guide, tells the lead exactly what happens next, and teases the next offer (the free intro session you'll build tomorrow).

Session 4 — Package it for the portfolio

≈ 1.5 hours

PORTFOLIOPortfolio #2: Lead-Gen Funnel

A working 2-step opt-in funnel with a real lead magnet, wired form, auto-tagging, and verified test submissions — plus 4 screenshots, a Loom walkthrough, and a case-study paragraph in your GHL Portfolio folder. Go to the home page and tick Portfolio #2 on the portfolio tracker.

📦 Deliverable of the day

The Ironworks — Gym Starter Guide funnel live at its preview URL, two test contacts in the CRM carrying the leadmagnet-optin tag, the PDF lead magnet, 4 screenshots, 1 Loom, 1 case-study paragraph, and a Day 9 journal entry.

⚠️ Watch out

If your test submission doesn't appear in Contacts, the usual culprits are: you previewed an unsaved version of the page, the form element has no form selected, or you tested the form inside the editor instead of on the preview URL. Always test in an incognito window on the real preview link.
