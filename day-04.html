Session 1 — The Conversations inbox

≈ 1.5 hours

Conversations is GHL's unified inbox: every email, SMS, call, voicemail, Facebook/Instagram DM, and WhatsApp message for the whole sub-account lands here, threaded per contact. Enter Summit Dental to start.

Session 2 — Email sending: LC Email vs dedicated domain

≈ 2 hours

GHL sends email through LC Email ("LeadConnector Email"), the built-in provider. Out of the box it sends from a shared LC subdomain — fine for testing, weak for deliverability. Production accounts add a dedicated sending domain (e.g. mail.summitdental.com) verified with DNS records. Today you set up what your situation allows and — more importantly — understand both.

Session 3 — Phone numbers, LC Phone & A2P 10DLC

≈ 2 hours

SMS in GHL runs on LC Phone (built on Twilio under the hood). Two things every specialist must understand: numbers cost real money (about $1–1.15/mo plus per-message fees, usually covered by trial credits), and in the US, business SMS legally requires A2P 10DLC registration before messages will deliver reliably.

Session 4 — Templates, snippets & merge fields

≈ 2 hours

Merge fields (GHL calls them "custom values" in the picker) personalize messages: {{contact.first_name}} becomes "Maria" at send time. Templates/snippets save canned messages the whole team reuses — and every workflow you build in Week 3 sends them.

📦 Deliverable of the day

A live communication stack in Summit Dental: LC Email verified working (test email delivered to your real inbox and reply threaded back), a phone number configured with call forwarding (or documented screenshots if purchase was blocked), 2 email templates and 2 SMS snippets with resolving merge fields, plus two screenshots in your portfolio folder and a journal entry containing your interview-ready SPF/DKIM/DMARC and A2P 10DLC explanations.

💡 Pro tip

Save the deliverability vocabulary from today in one journal block: LC Email, dedicated sending domain, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, LC Phone, A2P 10DLC, brand + campaign registration, sender reputation. Being fluent in these ten terms — even more than clicking skills — is what makes hiring managers believe "this person has actually shipped campaigns."

⚠️ Watch out

Only ever send tests to the Test Me contact (your own email/phone). Never send to the fake @example.com patients — bounces hurt your account's sending reputation — and never put anyone else's real number in a training account. Also: SMS costs credits per segment (~160 characters); keep tests short.
