How this program works

  • One page per day. Each day is broken into 2–4 timed sessions with numbered, click-by-click tasks. Check off each task as you finish it — your progress saves automatically in this browser.
  • Every day ends with a deliverable. A working funnel, a tested workflow, a screenshot, a Loom video. No day is "just watching."
  • Portfolio days are marked in gold. By Day 30 you'll have 13 real, demonstrable builds — the thing that actually gets you hired.
  • Don't skip, don't binge. The order is deliberate: each day builds on the last. If a day runs long, finish it before starting the next one.
