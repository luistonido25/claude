⚠️ Watch out

Three traps today: (1) the GHL Webhook action must be POST — a GET sends no body and n8n receives an empty item; (2) the n8n test listener stops after a couple of minutes — press Test workflow again right before each form submission; (3) after any change to the GHL workflow you must Save and re-Publish, or it keeps running the old version.
