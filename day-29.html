Day 29Positioning: Resume, LinkedIn & Freelance Profiles

By tonight you'll exist everywhere GHL hiring actually happens: a one-page resume built around your 13 builds, a keyword-loaded LinkedIn profile with your portfolio pinned, an Upwork profile with three ready-to-fire proposal templates, memberships in three communities where GHL jobs get posted daily — and a tracking sheet with a 10-applications-a-day quota that starts tomorrow.

0% of today's tasks complete

Session 1 — Define the offer, then write the resume

≈ 2 hours

You are not "looking for anything." You are a specialist with four interchangeable job titles, and every profile you write today uses their exact vocabulary — because recruiters and Upwork search boxes match keywords, not potential.

Session 2 — LinkedIn: profile as landing page

≈ 1.5 hours

Treat your LinkedIn like one of your funnels: headline = hook, About = offer, Featured = proof. Recruiters search LinkedIn for "GoHighLevel" daily and the candidate pool is thin — being findable is half the game.

Session 3 — Go where the GHL jobs actually are

≈ 1 hour

GHL hiring is weird: some of it is on LinkedIn and Indeed, but a huge share happens in Facebook groups, OnlineJobs.ph, and Upwork — agency owners posting "need a GHL VA, start Monday." You're going to set up camp in all of them today.

Session 4 — Upwork profile + three proposal templates

≈ 2 hours

Upwork rewards specificity and speed. Your profile answers "can this person do GHL work?" in five seconds, and your three saved templates let you send a personalized proposal in under ten minutes.

📦 Deliverable of the day

A one-page keyword-optimized resume PDF, a finished LinkedIn profile (headline, About, Featured with portfolio + 3 Looms, banner, 15+ skills, one announcement post), memberships in 3 Facebook groups plus saved searches with alerts on LinkedIn/Indeed/Upwork/OnlineJobs.ph, a complete Upwork profile with 3 saved proposal templates, and a tracking sheet with the 10-a-day quota written at the top — live from tomorrow.

💡 Pro tip

When a job post says "2+ years GHL experience required," apply anyway — with the line: "I won't pretend to have 2 years of client work. What I have is 150+ hours of intensive training and 13 working builds you can watch right now: [link]. Give me a test task and judge the output." Many "requirements" are wishes; a video of a working speed-to-lead system beats an unverifiable year of experience for a lot of agency owners.
