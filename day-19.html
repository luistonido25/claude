Session 3 — Portfolio #8: Database Reactivation (DBR)

≈ 2.5 hours

DBR is the classic "found money" campaign: instead of buying ads, you message the client's EXISTING list of lapsed customers with a compelling offer. It's often the first thing an agency runs for a new client because it produces revenue in week one. Structure: a Smart List finds the lapsed segment → a bulk-applied tag launches them into a 3-touch offer sequence → a goal exits anyone who replies or books → replies create opportunities in a dedicated pipeline.

⚠️ Watch out

Never promise a client you'll "filter out bad reviews" — that's review gating and it can get their Google Business Profile penalized. And never launch a real DBR without checking the list's consent status and A2P registration first: blasting SMS to a stale list is the fastest way to get a number flagged for spam. Saying these two things unprompted in an interview marks you as someone safe to hand client accounts to.
