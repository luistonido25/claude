Days 28–30 · Portfolio & Job Hunt ⏱ 6–7 hours

Day 28Package Your Portfolio

By tonight your 13 builds stop being a messy folder and become a single shareable link: audited assets, tight 2–4 minute Looms, five one-page case studies, and a master portfolio document that a hiring manager can skim in 90 seconds. Everything honestly framed as demonstration builds — because that framing, done confidently, is what makes you credible.

0% of today's tasks complete

Session 1 — Audit your folder against the 13-piece checklist

≈ 1.5 hours

You can't package what you haven't inventoried. Every portfolio piece needs, at minimum: 2+ screenshots, 1 Loom walkthrough, and (for the n8n builds, #10–#13) the exported workflow JSON. The five flagships (#5, #9, #11, #12, #13) also need a one-page case study — you'll write those in Session 3. Audit ruthlessly now so the rest of the day is pure production.

Session 2 — Re-record the weak Looms

≈ 2 hours

Looms are your #1 hiring asset — an employer will watch one video before they read anything. Every video graded B or C gets re-recorded today using this exact four-beat script. Target length: 2–4 minutes. One take is fine; two takes max. Done beats perfect.

Session 3 — One-page case studies for the 5 flagships

≈ 1.5 hours

A case study is a Loom for people who read. You need exactly five — for #5 speed-to-lead, #9 agency snapshot, #11 AI lead qualifier, #12 GHL ⇄ Sheets sync, #13 onboarding machine. One page each, same template, written in plain language. Non-negotiable rule: never invent client results. No "increased bookings 40%". These are demonstration builds on fictional businesses — say so, plainly, and describe what the system does, not metrics you don't have.

Session 4 — Assemble the master portfolio document

≈ 1.5 hours

One link to rule them all. This is the URL that goes in your resume, LinkedIn Featured section, and every proposal. Build it as a Google Doc or Notion page — whichever you'll actually maintain. Exact section order below; don't improvise the structure.

📦 Deliverable of the day

One shareable master portfolio link (Google Doc or Notion, incognito-tested) containing your intro, skills grid, and all 13 projects — every one with a legible screenshot and an A-grade 2–4 minute Loom, the five flagships each backed by a one-page case study PDF, and the four n8n JSON exports safely in your folder.

⚠️ The honesty rule (this one is permanent)

Summit Dental and Ironworks Fitness are fictional. Say so in every case study, every Loom, and the portfolio intro. Present the work as "demonstration builds" — never imply real clients, and never invent metrics ("boosted revenue 30%"). Agencies hire GHL specialists constantly and most have been burned by fake portfolios; a candidate who says "these are training builds, here they are working on video, I'll rebuild any of them live in your account" is rarer and more hireable than one more suspicious "results" claim.

🔗 Resources

  • Loom editor basics (trim, stitch): search support.loom.com for "trim your video".
  • Google Docs sharing: search Docs Help for "Share a file publicly" — you want "Anyone with the link — Viewer".
  • Notion alternative: notion.so — a free public page works fine as the master portfolio.
