Session 1 — What GHL actually is + start your account

≈ 2 hours

Before touching buttons, understand what you're operating. GoHighLevel (GHL) is an all-in-one platform agencies use to run marketing for local businesses: CRM, funnels, email/SMS, calendars, automations, and payments in one tool. Its core structural idea: your Agency account is the parent, and each client business lives in its own Sub-Account (also called a "Location").

Session 2 — Agency View tour

≈ 1.5 hours

Switch to Agency View (account switcher → click your agency name). Now walk the left sidebar top to bottom. For each item: click it, look around for 2–3 minutes, and write one sentence in your notes about what it's for.

Session 3 — Create your first sub-account + full sub-account tour

≈ 2.5 hours

You'll now create the fictional client you'll use all through Week 1: "Summit Dental", a local dental clinic. Every program exercise this week happens inside it.
