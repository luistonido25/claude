Week 2 · Funnels, Websites & Payments ⏱ 6–7 hours

Day 13Memberships & Courses

By tonight Ironworks will have a structured online course ("Kickstart Video Vault") with two weeks of lessons, a free offer and a $21 offer attached to it, and you'll have logged into the client portal as a member and completed a lesson yourself. Courses are how service businesses productize — and a surprisingly rare skill among GHL freelancers.

0% of today's tasks complete

Session 1 — Create the course & build its structure

≈ 2 hours

GHL's course engine lives under Memberships. The hierarchy mirrors the page builder's logic: a Product (the course) holds Categories (modules/weeks) which hold Lessons (called "Posts" in some versions). You'll use text lessons and placeholder videos — content quality isn't today's skill; structure is.

Session 2 — Offers: free preview + $21 paid

≈ 1.5 hours

Key mental model: courses aren't sold directly — Offers are. An offer is a purchasable wrapper that grants access to one or more products at a price. Same course, two doors: a free preview door and a $21 door.

Session 3 — Become your own member: grant, log in, complete

≈ 1.5 hours

You never ship a course you haven't experienced as a member. Grant yourself access through the free offer and walk the member journey end-to-end.

Session 4 — Drip vs all-at-once + the Week 3 bridge

≈ 1.5 hours

📦 Deliverable of the day

The Kickstart Video Vault course with 2 categories and 6 published lessons plus a thumbnail, a free offer and a $21 offer both published with working checkout links, yourself logged in as a member with one lesson completed, a drip test performed and reverted, 4 screenshots, and a Day 13 journal entry.

💡 Pro tip

When you pitch clients later, "membership site" scares them and "online course" sounds like work. Say instead: "a private members' area where your customers watch your content" — every gym, clinic, and coach immediately wants one. You built one in a day; most agencies charge $1,500+ for exactly this.
