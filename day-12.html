Session 1 — Connect Stripe in test mode

≈ 1.5 hours

Stripe has two parallel universes: live mode (real cards, real money) and test mode (fake cards, fake money, identical behavior). Everything today happens in test mode — you can "buy" things all day and no money moves anywhere.

Session 2 — Create the products

≈ 1.5 hours

Products are created once and attached to many things (order forms, invoices, payment links). You'll build Ironworks' three-product ladder: a $7 add-on, a $21 front-end offer, and a $49/mo core membership.

Session 3 — The order funnel: sales page, order form, bump & upsell

≈ 2.5 hours

The money funnel: sales page → order form (+ $7 bump) → one-click upsell ($49/mo) → thank you. This exact architecture powers most low-ticket offers on the internet, and you're about to own it.

Session 4 — Invoices & coupons

≈ 1 hour

Not every sale runs through a funnel. Gyms invoice for personal training blocks; agencies invoice for retainers. Two more tools and your payments toolkit is complete.

📦 Deliverable of the day

Stripe connected in test mode, three products ($21 Kickstart, $49/mo Membership, $7 Meal Plan), a 4-step order funnel with a working bump and one-click upsell, a completed $28 + $49/mo test purchase visible in Payments → Transactions, a sent-and-paid test invoice, a FOUNDER10 coupon, 4 screenshots, and a Day 12 journal entry.

⚠️ Watch out

Never test with a real card "because it's a small amount", and never build order forms on a client's live-mode connection. If the 4242 card is declined, that's your signal you're accidentally in live mode — stop immediately and fix the integration before touching anything else.
