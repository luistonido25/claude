Session 1 — Calendar concepts + create "New Patient Consult"

≈ 2 hours

GHL has three calendar-ish things — keep them straight: a calendar is one bookable event type ("New Patient Consult, 30 min"); a group bundles several calendars onto one page so the visitor picks a service; a service menu is a fancier storefront version of a group (used for salons/clinics with many services). You'll build calendars today and peek at groups.

Session 2 — Connect Google Calendar (conflicts + add-to)

≈ 1.5 hours

Two different sync jobs, two different settings: the conflict calendar (GHL checks your Google Calendar and hides slots where you're busy) and the add-to calendar (GHL writes new bookings INTO your Google Calendar). Both live on your user profile, not on the calendar itself.

💡 Pro tip

Always test booking pages in an incognito window. Logged-in GHL sessions can behave differently from what a real visitor sees (cached widgets, pre-filled data), and "works for me, broken for the client's customers" is an embarrassing bug class you can avoid with this one habit.

⚠️ Watch out

Don't confuse the two calendar places in the sidebar: Calendars (main sidebar) is the day-to-day appointment view where staff see and status bookings; Settings → Calendars is where calendars are built and configured. When a tutorial says "go to calendars", check which one it means — half of all beginner confusion on this topic is just that.
