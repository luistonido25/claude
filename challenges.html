How to use the Lab

  • Treat every brief as a paying client. Read it like a job post, write a one-paragraph proposal in your journal, THEN look at the architecture guide.
  • The guide tells you what to connect, not what to click. It names the exact nodes/modules and the flow — wiring them, mapping fields, and testing is your job. That's the skill that gets hired.
  • Done means demoed. A build counts when it passes the acceptance list, has a Loom walkthrough, and a one-page case study framed honestly as a demonstration build.
  • Filter by stack to match the tool you're currently sharpening, or hit 🎲 for a random unbuilt challenge.
  • Challenges 19–30 are full-combo client systems — CRM + funnels + automations in one engagement — and their guides are complete step-by-step solutions (every click, every node), so you can attempt solo first and then follow the build to the finish.
